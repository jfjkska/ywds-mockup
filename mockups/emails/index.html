Email 1Subject: Placement sessions for your children, and how to book

   

Placement session

Booking a placement session

Hi [Name],

Thank you for talking with me today. As we discussed, I'd like to meet your children for a placement session with each of them before we move forward. This will help us be confident that the Dyslexia Action Literacy Programme is the right fit for them both before you commit.

LengthApproximately 1 hour 30 minutes, one session per child.
WhereAt school, during the school day. I will arrange the time and date with the school.
Cost£50 per child (£100 for both), paid before I book the sessions with the school.

What happens in the session

I work 1-1 with your child through a set of short, relaxed activities covering reading, spelling and writing. I watch how your child approaches each task, which tells me about what they are struggling with. It isn't a test they can pass or fail, and they don't need to prepare anything. It will help me determine whether I think the intervention is suitable. It also identifies where in the programme is an appropriate place for them to begin their learning.

Booking

1Pay for the sessions

Pay for the sessions

The page shows £50 for one session. Change the quantity (Qty) to 2 so that it covers both children, and put both names in the box. You can pay by card, Apple Pay or Klarna.

2Fill in a short form about each child

Once you've paid, you'll go straight to a short form. It asks for each child's:

  • Full name and date of birth
  • School and year group
  • School contact (usually the SENDCo or class teacher), with their email address

The form also asks for your permission for me to contact the school to arrange the sessions.

What happens next

  1. Once I have your payment and the form, I contact the school and arrange a date. I'll email you as soon as it's booked.
  2. After the sessions, I'll email you with my findings and then a follow-up call free of charge to discuss.
  3. If the intervention is appropriate, sessions are weekly, an hour long, at school, using the Dyslexia Action Literacy Programme. These are £50 an hour, paid monthly or in blocks of six.
  4. If you'd also like a full written picture of how each child learns, the Learning Needs Assessment is still available (£300 per child). It isn't required for the intervention to begin.

If the support isn't the right fit for either child, I'll let you know and suggest alternatives.

1-1 literacy intervention

Starting your child's sessions

Hi [Name],

Thank you for talking with me, and for choosing 1-1 literacy support for [Child]. I'm really looking forward to working with them. Here is how the sessions work and the two quick steps to get started.

LengthOne hour a week
WhereAt school, during the school day. I arrange the time and a quiet room with school directly.
ProgrammeThe Dyslexia Action Literacy Programme (DALP)
Cost£50 an hour, paid in advance as a block of six, half a block, or monthly

How the sessions work

The first session includes a short placement screening, so the programme starts in exactly the right place for your child. It isn't a test they can pass or fail, and they don't need to prepare anything.

After that, each session follows a familiar shape, so your child knows what to expect:

  • Visual and auditory warm-ups
  • A review of patterns from previous sessions
  • One small, logical new concept
  • Structured reading and spelling practice using that concept
  • Talking about how they worked it out, so they learn which strategies help them
  • A game to finish

New learning is only introduced once the earlier learning is secure. I prepare resources for each session, liaise with school, and keep you updated on what we're working on and how things are going.

You can read more on my website: what the sessions are like, what's included and why I use DALP.

Email 4Subject: Booking your child's Learning Needs Assessment

   

Learning Needs Assessment

Booking your child's assessment

Hi [Name],

Thank you for talking with me, and for booking a Learning Needs Assessment for [Child]. Here is what it involves and the two quick steps to book.

LengthAbout two hours
WhereAt school, or another place we agree
ReportA detailed written report within a month
Cost£300, in full or in two payments of £150

What the assessment involves

Your child works one-to-one with me through a set of standardised tests covering reading, spelling, writing, memory and processing. As well as the scores, I watch how they approach each task, which gives a much fuller picture of how they learn.

The report sets out your child's strengths, what is getting in the way of their learning, and practical recommendations for home and school. It isn't a diagnosis of dyslexia, but it will tell you whether dyslexia is likely, and can point to other needs worth exploring.

You can read more on my website: Learning Needs Assessments.

Booking

1Choose how to pay

Pay in full · £300

One payment, before the assessment.

Pay £300

Pay in two parts · £150 now

£150 now, and the other £150 when the report is ready, before I send it to you.

Pay the first £150

You can pay by card, Apple Pay or Klarna.

2Fill in the short booking form

Once you've paid, you'll go straight to an online form. It is your booking and consent form, so there is nothing to print or sign. It asks for:

  • Your contact details and home address
  • Your child's name, date of birth, school and year group
  • Their SENDCo, with an email address
  • Anything I should know, such as health or other needs
  • Your consent, including whether I may share the report with school

What happens next

  1. Once I have your payment and the form, I contact school to arrange a date. I'll email you as soon as it's booked.
