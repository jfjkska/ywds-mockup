1Working with schools

Support in the school day, not on top of it

For an already overwhelmed and exhausted learner, extra support should feel like relief, not a penalty.

  1. Partnership

    I am eager to work in partnership with schools to deliver targeted 1:1 interventions.

  2. Fits the timetable

    By coordinating directly with school timetables, we can integrate these sessions seamlessly into the school day at a mutually convenient time.

  3. No burnout

    This ensures that students build core skills and confidence without the burnout of after-school or weekend work.

2Why literacy matters

Literacy underpins every school subject, and it's a life skill

Reading, writing, speaking and listening underpin almost all learning at school and in everyday life. With the right support, every child can progress.

~10%of children have dyslexia, around three in every classroom
£55bnestimated yearly cost of illiteracy to the UK economy
~57%of the adult prison population has literacy below the level expected of an 11-year-old

Early, targeted literacy support changes outcomes. Research consistently links education with significantly reduced reoffending.

Every child deserves the chance to read, write and thrive.

Sources: World Literacy Foundation; Ministry of Justice.

4About

Hello, I'm Natalie

I live in Pocklington, East Yorkshire, and I've worked with children and young people with special educational needs and disabilities for 15 years, across:

  • Primary, secondary and specialist settings
  • NHS children's speech and language therapy

I aim to empower children to take charge of their literacy challenges by teaching them to understand how they think and learn, and supporting them to identify the strategies that suit them best.
