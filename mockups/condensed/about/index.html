My background

How I became a dyslexia specialist

Natalie Fisher

I live in Pocklington, East Yorkshire. For 15 years I have worked across primary, secondary and specialist educational settings, supporting children and young people with SpLDs, autism, ADHD, SEMH needs, and severe and complex needs.

"I am passionate about literacy and believe everybody can learn and progress in the right environment with the right type of support."
Natalie Fisher

  1. Paired reader

    At secondary school I helped younger readers who found reading harder, because I have always loved it.

  2. Primary literacy assistant, then teaching assistant

    At university I ran story hubs to help Key Stage 1 and Key Stage 2 children improve their reading, and one of my first jobs gave me a taste for working in education.

  3. Youth work, then psychology

    I wanted to understand why some young people refused school and did not meet their targets.

  4. Assistant speech and language therapist

    I worked with children in the NHS for a couple of years.

  5. Educational psychology

    I worked for a Local Authority for several years.

  6. Emotional wellbeing and my own diagnosis

    I regularly saw children who were anxious about school because of their difficulties, then received my own dyslexia diagnosis, which spurred me on to complete my training.
