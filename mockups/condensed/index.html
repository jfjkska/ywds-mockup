Dyslexia specialist

Hello, I'm Natalie

I live in Pocklington, East Yorkshire, and I've worked with children and young people with special educational needs and disabilities for 15 years, across primary, secondary and specialist settings, and in NHS children's speech and language therapy and Local Authority educational psychology.

I aim to empower children to take charge of their literacy challenges by teaching them to understand how they think and learn, and supporting them to identify the strategies that suit them best.

What I do

Specialist literacy support, rooted in the Wolds

I support children and young people with literacy difficulties to overcome their barriers by identifying their specific needs and delivering structured, individually tailored intervention to help them develop self-belief, understand how they learn, and help them to become more independent learners.

Based in Pocklington and working with families within about 20 miles, including York, Market Weighton, Driffield and Beverley.

How I can help

Support built around your child

Learning Needs Assessment

A two-hour battery of standardised, psychometric tests to pinpoint strengths and needs and identify barriers to learning, with a detailed written report and practical recommendations within two weeks.

Not a diagnosis of dyslexia. Diagnostic assessments are planned for 2027: join the waiting list.

£300 or two instalments of £150
About the LNA

1-1 Literacy Intervention

Specialist 1-to-1 tuition: weekly, hour-long sessions delivered in person during the school day, using the structured, multi-sensory Dyslexia Action Literacy Programme (DALP), individually tailored for ages 7–16.

£50 / hour, paid monthly or in blocks

Why literacy matters

Literacy underpins every school subject, and it's a life skill

Reading, writing, speaking and listening underpin almost all learning at school and in everyday life. With the right support, every child can progress.

~10%of children have dyslexia, around three in every classroom

Early, targeted literacy support changes outcomes. Every child deserves the chance to read, write and thrive.

Source: British Dyslexia Association.
