Alt text

Paste into each image’s alt text field in Metricool (Instagram and Facebook both support it).

SlideAlt text
1Parents’ evening coming up? Worried about reading or spelling? A few notes beforehand can help you leave with a clearer idea of what happens next. Three steps: say, ask, leave with. Save this for the night.
2Step 1, say: set the agenda early. At the start: “I’m worried about reading and spelling. Could we focus on those tonight, please?” If you hear “they’re settled in class”: “At home, homework often ends in tears. Can we look at what’s making it so hard?” Bring one or two examples.
3Step 2, ask: questions to take with you. What can my child do confidently, and where do they need help? What reading or spelling support is already in place? What will we try next, and how will we know it’s helping? What would be useful to practise at home? When will we review this, and who do I contact meanwhile? No need to ask them all.
4Step 3, leave with: before you go, jot down what support will be tried, who will arrange it, and when you’ll review it. You’re not being a difficult parent by asking for clarity. Still unsure afterwards? Book a free consultation call at ywds.co.uk.

Scheduling in Metricool

  1. Check the brand’s time zone is Europe/London (Settings › Brand). The clocks go back at 02:00 that morning.
  2. Planning › Create post. Tick Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.
  3. Upload slides 1 to 4 (the 1080×1350 versions) in order. Set Instagram to a post (carousel), not a story or reel.
  4. Use “Customise per network” to paste each caption above. For TikTok, swap in the four TikTok images, add the title, and choose photo mode.
  5. Add the alt text to each image.
  6. Schedule for Sunday 25 October 2026, 10:00. Check all three appear in the planner at 10:00.
  7. Before 10:00, set the Instagram and TikTok link in bio to the blog post.
