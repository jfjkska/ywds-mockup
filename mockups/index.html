Yorkshire Wolds Dyslexia Service: homepage mockups

Two alternative designs for the homepage. The words are the same as the current homepage. Only the look, layout and structure have changed. Both aim to be more colourful and easier to read for people with dyslexia.

A: Meadow

Warm and sunny, but still calm and professional. It stays closest to the current site.

  • Lexend font, larger text, more space between lines
  • Colourful Wolds hills and a rainbow strip
  • Each service has its own colour and icon
  • The schools message gets its own highlighted box
  • Long reviews open with “Read the full review”
View Meadow

B: Crayon Box

Bolder and more playful. Big pastel blocks with one idea in each, and built-in reading tools.

  • Atkinson Hyperlegible font, with clear b/d and p/q shapes
  • Reading tools button: background tint, font, text size, spacing, a reading ruler and read-aloud
  • An “On this page” menu with numbered, colour-coded sections
  • Long paragraphs split into short numbered steps
  • Reviews shown as speech bubbles
View Crayon Box

Dyslexia-friendly changes in both designs

Based on the British Dyslexia Association's Dyslexia Style Guide:

  • Larger body text (18px rather than 16.5px) and 1.7 line spacing
  • Cream and pastel backgrounds, never pure white, and dark navy text, never pure black
  • No capital letters with wide spacing: the small uppercase labels are now in normal sentence case
  • Shorter lines of text, left-aligned, with no italics or justified text
  • Long blocks of text broken into shorter chunks, bullet points and steps
  • Headings, icons and colour work together, so colour is never the only clue
  • Bigger buttons and links, with a clear focus outline for keyboard users
