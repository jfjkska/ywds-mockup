About

Hello, I'm Natalie

I live in Pocklington, East Yorkshire, and I've worked with children and young people with special educational needs and disabilities for 15 years, across primary, secondary and specialist settings, and in NHS children's speech and language therapy and Local Authority educational psychology.

I aim to empower children to take charge of their literacy challenges by teaching them to understand how they think and learn, and supporting them to identify the strategies that suit them best.

More about me

Qualified and checked

  • Diploma in Specialist Teaching for Literacy Difficulties (Level 5)
  • Certificate of Competence in Educational Testing (Level 7)
  • MSc Psychology
  • Member of the British Psychological Society and the Dyslexia Guild
  • Enhanced DBS and professional indemnity insurance

Why literacy matters

Literacy underpins every school subject, and it's a life skill

Reading, writing, speaking and listening underpin almost all learning at school and in everyday life. With the right support, every child can progress.

~10%of children have dyslexia, around three in every classroom

Early, targeted literacy support changes outcomes. Every child deserves the chance to read, write and thrive.

Source: British Dyslexia Association.
